Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We've got answers. If you can't find what you're looking for, feel free to contact us.

Is BookLane free to use?

Yes, BookLane is completely free to download and use for listing, buying, and exchanging books. We believe in making education accessible and affordable for everyone.

What kind of books can I sell

You can sell a wide variety of books, including university textbooks, rare editions, classic literature, non-fiction, and more. You can list it in seconds.

How do I get paid for sale

BookLane facilitates the connection between buyers and sellers. You arrange the payment method directly with the buyer, whether it's cash on meetup or a digital transfer.

Is BookLane Available on IOS ?

The BookLane iOS app is coming soon! You can join the waitlist and be notified when we launch.
